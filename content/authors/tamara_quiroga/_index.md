**Tamara Quiroga Curín** is a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. Her research focuses on measuring social biases in large language models, aiming to develop more realistic and context-sensitive bias evaluations, with particular attention to how biases vary across different domains. She is currently investigating how LLMs interpret and respond to gender-inclusive language in Spanish and Italian.
